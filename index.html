About us

A studio built on
craft and clarity

CodeCanvas is a creative digital studio based in Kampala. We work with businesses across Uganda and East Africa who need websites, mobile apps, and design work that actually performs — not just looks good on a pitch deck.

The studio is led by Elijah Enrique, who handles design, development, and client strategy. We keep the team intentionally lean so that every project receives focused attention rather than being handed off to a junior after the first meeting.
